# Neue V5-Bildbauteile

Erstellt mit dem integrierten Bildgenerator auf transparentem Hintergrund. Das ursprüngliche Konzept war Stil- und Materialreferenz, kein Laufzeitbild. Anschließend wurden nur transparente Ränder beschnitten, proportional verkleinert und WebP-Dateien erzeugt. Die unverkleinerten Generierungen sind nicht Teil dieses Patches.

## bumper.webp

Single pinball pop-bumper GAME SPRITE on transparent background. Use the attached pinball concept as material and color reference only. Orthographic near top view, ONE circular mechanism centered, no board. Large translucent amber GOLD YELLOW acrylic mushroom cap, luminous golden rim, polished chrome circular socket and tiny screws, warm yellow insert, clear glossy physical depth. NOT speaker, NOT black/red/cyan disc. Object fills central 85% of square, no labels, no logos. Realistic detailed pinball machine part, same slight front elevation as concept.

## flipper.webp

Single LEFT pinball FLIPPER BAT game sprite on transparent background, exact orthographic TOP VIEW, horizontal to RIGHT. Ivory cream wide solid paddle with dark charcoal rubber rim, broad rounded heel pivot at far left with visible round screw, gently tapered rounded right tip. Length-to-maximum-width ratio 5:1, substantial opaque ivory playing face. No neon pink outline, no long decorative bars or bracket. Entire physical bat centered with small margins. Match detailed metal ivory pinball mechanical look of reference. No text, no logos, no other objects.

## sling.webp

One LEFT pinball SLINGSHOT COVER game sprite, transparent background. Reference pinball concept material style. Top view triangular substantial broad cover, vertices upper-left, bottom-left, bottom-right (right is inward point), triangle width:height about 0.85. Polished chromed border, three screw heads, translucent deep magenta red acrylic main surface with luminous RED PINK chevron slashes filling the face, dark mechanical backing. A broad real triangular pinball slingshot, no tall thin shard, no background, no text, no other parts.

## hologram.webp

Single central CYBERPUNK PINBALL HOLOGRAM PROJECTOR game sprite transparent background, matching attached concept. A LARGE ROUND broad physical chrome and gunmetal cylindrical pedestal seen nearly from above with slight front elevation, yellow illuminated vertical columns and warm gold light ring all around. Above it a bright cyan transparent holographic miniature CITY with skyscrapers, and one prominent vertical glowing magenta portrait screen containing a fictional cyberpunk woman. No text or letters on portrait. Pedestal circumference round, no octagonal plinth. Visible depth, screws and brackets. Narrow dark horizontal intake slot in front base, not an open circular hole. Object fills image, aspect about 0.85 width to height. No board, no ball, no brands, no NC, no 2077, no Night City.

No brands, no NC, no 2077, no other words. Detailed physical realistic pinball cabinet header as in reference, straight-on front view, not full pinball machine, no playfield, no legs.
